Market Regime & Dealer Gamma Impact

The NQU2026 options structure operates within a net-positive gamma regime, anchored just above the Zero-Gamma Flip level of 29,350. In this configuration, options market makers are net-short gamma and maintain long underlying index/futures delta hedges to neutralize their books.


This creates a mechanical, mean-reverting intraday feedback loop: as spot pushes toward the 29,600 minor call wall, dealers aggressively sell underlying futures into rallies to trim excess delta. Conversely, as price dips toward the 29,300 minor put wall, dealers buy underlying futures to replenish declining delta.


Structural Vulnerability: A sustained breach below the 29,350 Zero-Gamma Flip transforms dealers from a stabilizing force into a pro-cyclical source of momentum.
