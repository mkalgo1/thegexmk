Executive Overview & Gamma Regime

Primary Market State: Positive Gamma Regime (Net Positive GEX ~+$601M per 1% move across front-week expiration).


Structural Mechanics: The QQQ options complex reflects the underlying tech-heavy Nasdaq-100 footprint, operating in a long gamma regime. Market makers are net long calls overall, meaning dealer hedging flows actively damp volatility.


Upside Hedging: As QQQ moves up toward overhead call walls, dealers sell underlying shares/futures to lock in gains and stay delta-neutral.


Downside Hedging: As QQQ drops toward high-volume put strike clusters, dealers buy underlying shares/futures to absorb downside momentum.


Execution Environment: Expect range-bound, mean-reverting price action with lower intraday volatility unless a major catalyst forces short-covering above the primary Call Wall.
