Zero Gamma Flip Strategy Protocol

Above Gamma Flip (~29,000+)
Strategy: Fade extreme moves at high Call GEX levels.

Below Gamma Flip (~28,950-)

Execution: Dealer short gamma forces market makers to sell into falling prices, expanding bid-ask spreads and driving rapid legs down toward the lower Put Walls.
